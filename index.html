    Bio

    I'm an animator and commercial director based in SLC, UT who paints a lot.

    I love it when I have the opportunity to direct a project, especially when I am also involved in the art direction. I try to have an attitude going into projects of "what can I do visually to help this?" As a trained illustrator I've found it really challenging but also rewarding to show these strengths off when I can in a project.

    In the end, my art most likely won't be featured in the finished product but it's great to know that it helped. Winning bids knowing that I had a hand in making our pitch more exciting is something I am very proud of, whether it be from storyboards, animatics, or splash illustrations. Sometimes, those projects I start I actually get to direct and see to the finish line!

    Getting to do both is like holding two wires close to each other and watching the electric sparks fly between them. Let's make some sparks fly!
